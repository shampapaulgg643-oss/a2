Our kitchen

Hello from the Artichoke Plum kitchen

We are a small team of home cooks who believe the best meals start with good ingredients and a relaxed approach, not complicated techniques.

Why we started

A site for people who like cooking, but are short on time

Artichoke Plum grew out of a shared notebook. Friends kept asking the same questions: what can I do with this squash, how do I stop my roast vegetables going soggy, is it worth buying artichokes at all? We started writing the answers down, and the notebook slowly turned into this website.

The name comes from two ingredients that sum up our cooking year. Artichokes mark the start of spring and plums the end of summer. Between them sits everything else worth cooking.

We are not a restaurant and we are not chefs. We cook in ordinary kitchens with ordinary ovens, and we write for people doing the same.

Seasonal first

Every recipe starts with what is actually good right now, so the ingredients do most of the work.

Tested at home

We cook each recipe several times in a regular kitchen before it goes on the site, and we note what went wrong.

Plainly written

Clear quantities in cups and grams, realistic timings and no unnecessary jargon. Cooking should feel approachable.

What you will find here

Practical help, not perfection

  • Recipes built around one or two seasonal ingredients, mostly ready in under an hour.
  • A seasonal guide explaining how to choose, store and prepare produce throughout the year.
  • A produce calendar on our homepage that shows what is at its best each month.
  • Kitchen basics like conversion tables and simple techniques that make everyday cooking easier.

We keep things free to read. The site may show advertising to cover running costs, but we never let advertisers influence what we write. You can read more in our Editorial Policy.

A note on health claims

We write about food, not medicine

You will not find miracle ingredients or diet promises here. We talk about flavour, texture and technique, and we leave nutrition and medical advice to qualified professionals.

If you have allergies or specific dietary needs, please read every ingredient list carefully and check the labels on the products you buy.
