Cookie Policy

What cookies are, which ones Artichoke Plum uses, and how to control them.

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Artichoke Plum uses cookies and similar technologies on www.artichokeplum.com. Please read it together with our Privacy Policy.

What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file saved on your computer, phone or tablet when you visit a website. Cookies let a website recognise your device and remember information about your visit, such as your preferences. Some cookies are placed by the website you are visiting (first-party cookies), and some by other services the website uses (third-party cookies).

Cookies we use

Strictly necessary

These cookies and similar browser storage are needed for basic functions, such as remembering whether you have accepted or declined our cookie notice. They do not collect information used for marketing and cannot be switched off in our systems.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to collect anonymous, aggregated information about how visitors use the site, such as which recipes are viewed most and how people find us. Typical cookies include _ga and _ga_<ID>, which can last up to two years. This helps us decide which guides and recipes to write next.

Advertising

If advertising is shown on this site, Google and other third-party vendors may set cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites, limit the number of times you see an ad and measure ad performance. You can manage personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings.

How to control cookies

When you first visit, our cookie notice lets you accept all cookies or keep to essential ones only. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. Blocking all cookies may stop some parts of the website from working as expected.

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Updates

We may update this Cookie Policy when we add or remove tools or when the law changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
