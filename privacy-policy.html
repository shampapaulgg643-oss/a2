Privacy Policy

How Artichoke Plum collects, uses and protects information when you read our recipes, join the Sunday List or contact us.

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Artichoke Plum (“we”, “us”, “our”) runs www.artichokeplum.com, a free seasonal cooking and recipe website. We take your privacy seriously and collect as little personal information as we reasonably can. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices available to you.

Information you give us

When you use our contact form, we receive your name, email address, the subject you choose and your message. When you join the Sunday List, we receive your email address. We use this information only to reply to you or to send the newsletter you asked for, and we never ask for payment details because nothing on this website is for sale.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we automatically receive certain technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser and device type, operating system, referring website, pages viewed and the date and time of your visit. This helps us understand which recipes and guides are useful and keep the site running smoothly. Much of this is collected through cookies, explained in our Cookie Policy.

How we use information

  • To answer questions and feedback sent through our contact form or by email.
  • To send the Sunday List newsletter to people who have subscribed.
  • To measure how the website is used and improve our recipes, layout and guides.
  • To protect the website against spam, abuse and security threats.
  • To comply with legal obligations where necessary.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google LLC, to understand how visitors use our site. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect information such as how often you visit and which pages you view. Google’s use of this data is governed by its own privacy policy, described at How Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can prevent collection by installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Advertising

This website may display advertisements served by Google and other third-party vendors. These vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to show ads based on your visits across the internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies at www.aboutads.info/choices.

Sharing information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us operate the website, such as our hosting company and email service, and only as needed for them to do that work. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our readers and our website.

Data retention

Contact messages are kept for as long as needed to respond and handle any follow-up, and then deleted within a reasonable period. Newsletter email addresses are kept until you unsubscribe. Analytics data is retained according to our Google Analytics settings and is reviewed periodically.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to object to or restrict how we use it. California residents have rights under the CCPA and visitors from the EU and UK have rights under the GDPR. To make a request, contact us using the details below. You can unsubscribe from the Sunday List at any time using the link in every email.

Children

Artichoke Plum is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will remove it promptly.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted HTTPS connections and limited access to our systems, to protect the information we hold. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

External links

Our pages may link to other websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites and recommend reading their privacy policies before sharing information with them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally to reflect changes in our practices or the law. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.
