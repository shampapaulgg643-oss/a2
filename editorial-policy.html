Editorial Policy

How we develop, test, write and correct the recipes and guides on Artichoke Plum.

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Readers trust us to give them recipes that work and information that is accurate. This page explains the standards we hold ourselves to when creating content for Artichoke Plum.

How recipes are developed

Every recipe begins with a seasonal ingredient and a simple question: what is the easiest way to make this taste its best? We write a first draft, cook it in a normal home kitchen, adjust the quantities and timings, and cook it again. A recipe is only published once it has worked reliably at least twice.

How we write

  • Ingredients are listed in the order they are used, in US cups with gram or millilitre equivalents where helpful.
  • Methods describe what food should look, smell or feel like, not only how long to cook it.
  • We mention common mistakes and useful substitutions based on our own testing.
  • We avoid exaggerated claims and never describe foods as cures or treatments for any condition.

Accuracy and sources

Seasonal information, storage advice and food-safety guidance are based on our cooking experience and checked against reputable public sources such as government food-safety agencies and agricultural extension services. Where advice varies by region, we say so.

Corrections

If we get something wrong, we want to know. Readers can report errors through our contact page. We review every report, correct confirmed mistakes promptly and update the page date when a significant change is made.

Independence and advertising

Artichoke Plum is free to read and may be supported by display advertising. Advertisers have no say in what we write, which recipes we publish or which ingredients we recommend. We do not accept payment for positive coverage, and any future sponsored content would be clearly labelled as such.

Images

We use photographs from Unsplash under the Unsplash License to illustrate ingredients and dishes. We choose images that honestly represent the food being discussed and describe them with accurate alt text for accessibility.
